# Communication Style
Audience: youth and community groups in Colombia, with event-specific variations.
Default language: friendly natural Colombian Spanish, concise, readable and respectful.
WhatsApp: short paragraphs, clear next action, few emojis and no pushy tone.
Social: clear opening hook, essential facts, accessible hashtags only if useful.
Invitation: what/why/when/where, with TBD for missing logistics.
Civic/political communication: transparent factual descriptions and respectful voluntary participation; avoid manipulating or inferring political preferences.
Never turn suggestions, sample dates or locations into confirmed claims.
